# Keyword research — chatform.in

Researched 2026-09-18 with the superseo `keyword-deep-dive` method: the top
results for each keyword were read in full, not guessed from titles. Search
tools used returned ranked links only, so AI Overviews, People Also Ask and
featured snippets could not be observed directly — anything about them below
is inferred and says so. Competitor facts are from the vendors' own pages.

**Where we start:** chatform.in is ~4 weeks old, has no backlinks and is not
yet indexed by Google — unranked for everything. Four other products share the
name (chatform.com, .co, .org, .site). See `docs/LINK-BUILDING.md`.

## Summary

| Keyword | Intent | Difficulty | Head-term timeline | Winnable first (3–6 months) | Our page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| typeform alternative | Commercial investigation | Hard | 12–24 months | "free typeform alternative unlimited responses", "typeform alternative with AI follow-up", "conversational typeform alternative" | `/typeform-alternative` |
| typeform alternatives | Commercial investigation (listicles) | Hard | 12–24 months | same long tail | `/blog/typeform-alternatives` |
| jotform alternative(s) | Commercial investigation | Hard | 12–24 months | "jotform alternative with no submission limit", "jotform ai agents alternative", "jotform alternative conversational" | `/jotform-alternative` |
| ai form builder | Commercial / transactional | Hard | 12–24 months | "ai form that asks follow-up questions", "ai survey with follow-up questions" | none yet → `/ai-form-builder` |

**What is true of all three:** the thing that sets chatform apart — reading the
answer and following up when it is thin, answering the respondent's questions,
going back for people who leave — is missing from every top-ranking page. The
head terms need authority (referring domains, and above all being named in the
third-party lists that already rank); on-page work alone will not move a
four-week-old domain past Jotform, Tally or Zapier.

## 1. "typeform alternative" / "typeform alternatives"

**SERP.** Singular: about half single-vendor "X vs Typeform" pages (Jotform,
Tally, Formbricks, Fillout), half listicles (Qualaroo, Formgrid,
typeformalternative.com, Antforms). Plural: mostly listicles (involve.me,
Fluent Forms, Qualaroo, MakeForms, Formgrid) with Jotform, Formbricks and
Fillout still present. Five of nine URLs appear in both, so Google treats them
as largely one topic. Nearly every title carries "(2026)" — moderate freshness
pressure. Zero-click risk low–medium (people click to compare).

**Top 3 (singular)**

| URL | Authority | Format | Words | Angle | Best at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| jotform.com/typeform-alternative | Major brand | Product landing | ~900 | "More features, more power" | Trust: G2 4.7, logos, HIPAA/PCI. No table, no FAQ |
| tally.so/help/tally-a-free-typeform-alternative | Strong SaaS | 1-vs-1 | ~3,500 | Free & unlimited | 75+ row feature table, ratings, 11 FAQs |
| formbricks.com/typeform-alternative | Mid, open source | Long 1-vs-1 | ~5,500 | Open source, self-hosted | TLDR table, "Why users leave Typeform", verdict per section, named CEO author + date, 9 FAQs, "Other alternatives" |

Plural benchmark: involve.me (~5.5–6k words) — quick answer per use case,
"How we tested", "Excluded and why", dated pricing table, named author with a
conflict disclosure, 8 FAQs.

**Gaps:** nobody covers answer quality; nobody explains that Typeform's
conversational product (Formless) is sold separately; nobody covers what
happens to people who leave halfway. The pages that do cover conversational
forms are niche AI sites ranking for the long tail, not the head term.

**Which page targets what:** "typeform alternative" → `/typeform-alternative`
(1-vs-1). "typeform alternatives" → `/blog/typeform-alternatives` (listicle).
Cannibalisation risk is moderate: keep both, make the titles clearly different,
and cross-link them in body copy with exact anchors.

**Edits**
- `/typeform-alternative` — title "Free Typeform Alternative: Conversational
  Forms · chatform"; meta ≤160 chars; add "At a glance", "Why people leave
  Typeform", "Switching from Typeform", "Other Typeform alternatives"; FAQs for
  "free Typeform alternative" and "open-source Typeform alternative"; named
  author + last-checked date.
- `/blog/typeform-alternatives` — title "Best Typeform Alternatives (2026):
  7 Compared"; add Formbricks as the open-source pick (facts from
  formbricks.com); a "best for" column; a quick answer by use case near the top;
  "How I compared them", "What to look for", "Can I import my Typeform forms?"
  and a FAQ with FAQPage schema; link to `/typeform-alternative`.

**Timeline:** 90 days — both indexed, top 30 for the long tail, outside the top
50 for the head terms. Head terms: 12–24 months with authority work.

## 2. "jotform alternative" / "jotform alternatives"

**SERP.** Singular and plural return nearly the same top 10: 7 of 10 are
listicles (Zapier, Checkbox, Zonka, Fluent Forms, Zite …), 3 are 1-vs-1 vendor
pages (Fillout /vs/jotform, Tally help, Tripetto). AlternativeTo also ranks.

**Top 3**

| URL | Authority | Format | Words | Angle | Best at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| zapier.com/blog/jotform-alternatives | Very high | Listicle, 9 tools | ~3,500 | "Jotform does everything; specialists do one thing well" | Stated testing method; best-for table. Names Typeform "best for conversational forms" |
| checkbox.com/blog/best-jotform-alternatives | Mid, vendor | Listicle + table + FAQ | ~4,500 | Enterprise, HIPAA, self-hosting | "Why teams leave Jotform"; free plan / entry price / HIPAA / self-host columns |
| fillout.com/vs/jotform | Mid-high, vendor | 1-vs-1 | ~3,500–4,000 | "Modern Jotform alternative" | Free-plan table; one-click Jotform import; "Jotform may be better when…" |

**What searchers are escaping** (from ranking pages and review snippets), most
frequent first: price as usage grows; free-plan caps (per Jotform's own pricing
page, read 2026-09-18: Starter = 5 forms, 100 submissions/mo, 10,000 views/mo,
100 MB, 1 user; Bronze $34/mo yearly, 25 forms, 1,000 submissions); single-user
plans (multi-user is Enterprise only); dated builder UX; HIPAA only from Gold
($99/mo yearly).

**Our page** covers price, caps and complexity, and is candid about Jotform AI
Agents (an edge no top page has). It is silent on seats, the 5-form limit as a
headline, form-view limits and migrating. It is thinner (~2,100 words) than the
top 3, and its meta description is over 200 chars.

**Decision:** no separate Jotform listicle yet — a new domain will not beat
Zapier or Checkbox with one. Add a short "other Jotform alternatives" section to
the 1-vs-1 page instead. Revisit around month 6.

**Edits** — title "Jotform Alternative: Conversational Forms, Free Plan"; meta
≤160; "Why people leave Jotform"; "Moving from Jotform" (honest: no importer);
free-plan table; other-alternatives section; FAQs ("with no submission limits",
"free Jotform alternative", "import my Jotform forms?", "best for teams").

**Timeline:** 90 days — positions 40–80 for the head terms, top 20 for the long
tail. Top 10: 12–24 months, mostly by getting into the third-party roundups.

## 3. "ai form builder" (+ "ai form generator", "free ai form builder", "ai survey builder")

**SERP.** Jotform (/ai/form-builder), Fluent Forms, Google Workspace
Marketplace, MakeForms, Feathery, Typeform (/ai), Zite's listicle, Makeform.ai,
Formester; variants add Fillout, involve.me, forms.app, Fomr, FormHug,
Formshare. The ranking listicles are all vendor-written. Likely (inferred) an
AI Overview and marketplace listings. Stable, evergreen.

**Top 3**

| URL | Authority | Format | Words | Angle | Best at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| jotform.com/ai/form-builder | Very high | Landing + 12 FAQs | ~4,500 | AI Agents; "free to use" | Breadth, 70+ internal links, logos, ratings |
| typeform.com/ai | Very high | Feature showcase | ~850 | Forms → automations → insights; "Clarify with AI" asks up to 2 follow-ups | Brand; closest overlap with chatform's respondent-side AI |
| fillout.com/ai-form-builder | High | Tool landing + 6 FAQs | ~850 | Make a form with AI; import from PDF / Google Forms | Tight answers to "is it free / quiz / from a PDF?" |

**Gaps:** the respondent side is almost absent; nobody shows the same form as
generated fields *and* as the conversation a respondent has; nobody covers
answering respondents from uploaded knowledge; nobody is honest about when
generated output needs editing; free-plan caps are vague everywhere.

**Decision:** GO on a dedicated `/ai-form-builder` page now, angled at the
respondent side ("keeps working after you publish"); no chasing the head term
directly. It is a secondary page, so it does not break the home page's
"conversational forms" positioning — it argues generation is table stakes and
the conversation is the difference. Cover "AI survey builder" in its copy
rather than a second page.

**Winnable first:** "AI form that asks follow-up questions" / "AI survey with
follow-up questions" — mostly help-centre articles (Typeform, Lyssna, Maze,
Wondering) and niche tools (BlockSurvey, Diaform).

**Page spec:** title "AI Form Builder That Asks Follow-Up Questions | chatform";
H1 "An AI form builder that keeps working after you publish."; sections — draft
from a sentence or URL (with free/Pro caps), the same form twice, follow-ups on
thin answers, answers from your knowledge (Pro), what the AI won't do,
drafting-vs-conversation comparison (vendor-sourced), free plan in numbers
(from `plans.ts`), FAQ; named author, date, link to `/conversational-forms`.

**Timeline:** 90 days — indexed, page 2–3 for the follow-up variants, not in the
top 100 for "ai form builder".

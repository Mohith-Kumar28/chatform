# chatform launch film: script and storyboard (as built)

63.9s, 1920×1080, 60fps. Music: "Take This Higher" (Mixkit, Mixkit Stock Music Free License), 124.0 BPM, used from its bar 2. Drops on beats 16, 52 and 100; the breakdown runs from beat 84 to 100.

## The script (one line per scene, read it on its own)

1. Ever filled out a long Google Form? 25 questions. All required.
2. Nobody finishes them.
3. The few who finish leave you this.
4. chatform. Conversational forms people actually finish.
5. Three reasons you get better answers.
6. (1 of 3, Better questions) One question at a time. Thin answer? It asks again. And records what it means.
7. It understands what people type.
8. It answers their questions too.
9. (2 of 3, Follow-ups) It brings back the ones who left.
10. (3 of 3, Proof it worked) Hold some back. See the difference.
11. Read the conversation, not the row.
12. Setting it up? One sentence.
13. Describe it. Get the whole form.
14. Change it by saying so.
15. Then put it anywhere.
16. Plus the parts every form needs.
17. The result: answers you can use.
18. Free forever. Unlimited responses. No card. Start today.
19. chatform. Conversational forms people actually finish. Start free at chatform.in.

## Claims shown, and where they come from

- The three reasons are the landing page's own pillars ("Three pillars behind more submissions": better questions, automated follow-ups, proof it worked).
- Asks again, understands free text, knowledge base answers, follow-up emails at 4h / 1 day / 3 days, held-back comparison, "Read the conversation, not the row", prompt and URL generation, embed modes, API: the landing page and `apps/web/src/app/.well-known/brand-facts.json/route.ts`.
- 27 question types, 19 operators, verified respondents, webhooks, CSV, brand: brand-facts and the plan cards.
- Free forever, unlimited responses, no card: the pricing section and `freePlanRequiresCard: false`.
- The spreadsheets, Maya's journey and the dot grids are illustrations, not measured results; no percentages are shown.

## Deliberately left out

- The Slack answer from the site's demo script (brand-facts lists no Slack integration).
- Any completion-rate number (no published benchmark).
